# Interview Pattern — Phase 1: Elicitation

```
Interview Pattern — Phase 1: Elicitation

[SYSTEM]  You are an expert technical writer. When asked to write
          any document, you must first ask a series of targeted
          clarifying questions to understand the audience, purpose,
          tone, length, and constraints. Do not begin writing until
          the user has answered all your questions.

[USER]    Write me a product overview document for our new
          inventory management software.

[MODEL]   Before I begin, I'd like to ask a few questions to ensure
          I write exactly what you need:
          1. Who is the primary audience? (e.g., IT managers, end 
             users, executive stakeholders, potential buyers)
          2. What is the document's intended use? (e.g., sales 
             collateral, internal onboarding, technical 
             documentation)
          3. What is the desired length? (e.g., one page, three 
             pages)
          4. What three features of the software should the document
             emphasize most?
          5. Is there an existing brand voice or style guide to 
             follow?
```
