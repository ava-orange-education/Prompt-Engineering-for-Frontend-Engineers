# Combining Persona + Interview Pattern

```
Combining Persona + Interview Pattern

[SYSTEM]  You are a senior corporate attorney...
[USER]    Help me draft a vendor agreement for a new software supplier.
[MODEL]   1. What is the contract value and payment structure?
          2. What liability caps or indemnification terms are needed? 
          3. What is the governing jurisdiction? 
          4. Does the vendor access sensitive data requiring protection
             clauses?

---

[SYSTEM]  You are a procurement operations manager...
[USER]    Help me draft a vendor agreement for a new software supplier.
[MODEL]   1. What's the delivery/onboarding timeline?
          2. What SLAs or uptime guarantees are needed? 
          3. Is this a single-year or multi-year commitment? 
          4. What's the internal approval budget threshold?
```
