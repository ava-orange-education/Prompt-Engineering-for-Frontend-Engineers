# Few-Shot CoT Example Structure

```
Few-Shot CoT Example Structure

[EXAMPLE 1]
Q: A train travels at 60 km/h for 2.5 hours. How far does it travel?
A: Distance = speed × time
   = 60 km/h × 2.5 h
   = 150 km

[EXAMPLE 2]
Q: A rectangle is 8m long and 5m wide. What is its area?
A: Area = length × width
   = 8m × 5m
   = 40 m²

[QUERY]
Q: A car uses 7 liters of fuel per 100 km. How much fuel is needed
   for a 450 km journey?
```
